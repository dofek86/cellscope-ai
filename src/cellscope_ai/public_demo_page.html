How it works

A compact research pipeline

01
Image goes in

The model accepts bright-field microscopy images. Provide only images you are permitted to upload.

02
Features are compared

Frozen DINOv2 analyzes global and local views; same-session images can contribute context.

03
A reviewer decides

The output helps prioritize attention. It does not establish a verified cause for a poor image.

Research use only

A high score does not clear an experiment or diagnose a condition. New laboratory sessions have not yet been independently validated. Your uploads are held temporarily for the request and then removed; they are not used for training.

The evidence behind the demo

Built to show the signal—and its limits.

The source dataset carries a binary good/bad label, not a confirmed reason for every rejected frame. We report grouped development performance, known errors and the next scientific test rather than presenting a model score as a biological verdict.

Session-aware evaluation. Whole acquisition sessions were held out in development instead of mixing neighboring frames at random.
Second split. The same images yielded 79.81% balanced accuracy with a different grouping; this is not an independent cohort.
Next step. Freeze the model and test new chips, operators and acquisition sessions with expert reason labels.
